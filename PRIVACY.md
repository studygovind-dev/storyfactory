# Privacy Policy - StoryBite TV uploader

This small tool belongs to the StoryBite TV channel and is used only by the channel owner.
It uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data from viewers or other users.
- The only data it handles is the owner's own YouTube login token, kept privately in the owner's
  GitHub repository secrets, and used only to upload the owner's videos.
- Contact: storybitetv2@gmail.com
